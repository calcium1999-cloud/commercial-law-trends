# 商业法律研究动向

**报告周期**：2026-10-12 — 2026-10-14
**数据来源**：ECGI | Harvard | OBLB | ProMarket | CLS | Yale JREG | Jotwell | BWI | NY Fed | ECB | BoE

## 📊 本周研究趋势概括

本期十一大来源共新增12篇文章，来自Harvard、OBLB、ProMarket与CLS。按交叉标签计算，公司治理6篇、其他5篇、金融监管4篇、科技、数据与AI 1篇。整体研究脉络并非单纯围绕新规则展开，而是集中追问三个相互关联的问题：规则由谁制定、公司可以保留多大自治空间，以及制度变化后责任最终由谁承担。
Rule 14a-8 构成本期最密集的议题群。SEC拟彻底废除股东提案规则，使原本由联邦证券法提供的参与渠道面临制度性收缩；Harvard的公司选择分析、代理权征集季数据与影子SEC声明分别从私人秩序、经验趋势和规范正当性三个层面回应这一变化。几篇文章共同提示，若联邦统一底线退出，股东提案机制可能转而取决于公司章程、州公司法和市场压力，治理权利因而更易出现公司间分化。
公司内部问责的另一条主线连接高管薪酬、高管义务与注册地竞争。罗素3000与标普500薪酬数据呈现董事会激励设计的现实结构；高管义务研究则指出，真正支配日常经营、风险与合规的人并未始终受到与其权力相称的责任审查。特拉华州AI公司构想进一步把这一问题推向前沿：当决策被交给算法或无明确人格的系统时，司法辖区若继续以降低责任吸引企业，可能同时削弱可识别的人类问责主体。
监管工具的选择同样呈现由硬法向解释、执法裁量与税收定性的分化。结构性票据披露函显示，监管机关需要澄清非正式文件与既有工作人员指导之间的效力边界；司法部修订《司法手册》则体现执法政策如何在不修改制定法的情况下收窄《虚假申报法》的适用路径。附带权益税收争议提醒我们，经济利益的法律定性本身就是分配选择，不能因为其表现为技术性税制问题而误归为公司治理。
小型农场破产制度以及两篇经济思想史文章把视野扩展到制度设计的社会基础。农业企业的季节性、土地依赖与公共粮食安全功能说明，通用的小企业破产框架未必足以处理特定产业风险；芝加哥传统与自由主义的历史论述则展示，市场自由和公共利益并非必然对立。综合本期材料，值得持续关注的不是监管与自治之间的抽象二选一，而是不同制度如何配置参与权、裁量权与可追责性。

#### Delaware’s AI Corporation and the New Race to the Bottom
**作者**：Igho Dabor
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：For most of the last century, Delaware was the default home for American incorporation. That is now changing. After the Court of Chancery struck down Elon Musk’s multibillion-dollar Tesla pay package in Tornetta , citing his undue influence over the board, companies began relocating their legal home—a trend now known as ‘ DExit ’. By August 2026, about eighty companies had announced plans to leave, most for Nevada or Texas . The Delaware Supreme Court reinstated the package in December 2025, in re Tesla , holding rescission an improper remedy, but it did not dispute the board’s lack of independence, and by then the exodus was already underway. Texas and Nevada compete on familiar terms, Texas with a new specialist Business Court and Nevada with an even more management-friendly code . Most reincorporations cluster among founder-led firms in technology, crypto, and artificial intelligence , the very companies Delaware’s latest proposal aims to attract. The competition is no longer about incorporation costs but about how much accountability a jurisdiction can avoid. Delaware’s response goes furthest, proposing to remove the person who could be held accountable. The real issue is not that firms are leaving, but what they are moving toward. In 2025, Texas amended its Business Organisations Code to make directors harder to sue, requiring shareholders to hold three percent of shares before a derivative suit, unlike Delaware .

#### Designing insolvency systems for small farm enterprises: beyond micro and small enterprise insolvency frameworks
**作者**：Virginia Torrie
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：Small, owner-operated farms represent a distinct category of small enterprise that warrants specialized treatment under insolvency law. In many emerging economies, their collective importance to livelihoods, rural communities and food security makes agricultural financial distress a matter of broader public concern. Effective reform requires both tailoring general small enterprise insolvency principles to agricultural realities and developing a supportive institutional ecosystem in which those rules can work. International initiatives have recognized that conventional insolvency proceedings are often too complex and expensive for micro and small enterprises. The World Bank’s Principles for Effective Insolvency and Creditor/Debtor Regimes and UNCITRAL’s Legislative Guide on Insolvency Law for Micro- and Small Enterprises provide an important foundation: accessible procedures, out-of-court restructuring, simplified formal proceedings, debt discharge, stigma reduction and institutional capacity. My recent article in the International Insolvency Review argues that small farm enterprises require both refinement of these principles and supplementary principles directed specifically at agriculture. The scale of agricultural employment underscores this broader importance. In 2023, agriculture (including forestry and fishing) employed 916 million people worldwide—26.1 per cent of total employment .
