# 商业法律研究动向

**报告周期**：2026-10-09 — 2026-10-12

## 📊 本期研究按语

本期11篇文章呈现出一条共同线索：制度效果不仅取决于规则文本，还取决于规则如何转化为组织行为、市场激励与利益分配。可持续披露、薪酬投票、AI风险管理与企业集团竞争问题，分别揭示了信息、监督和市场结构之间的联系；研究重心由“是否设定规则”进一步转向“规则通过何种机制发挥作用”。

公司治理研究突出披露与行动之间的反馈。董事ESG义务论文指出，报告并非单纯的信息输出，可靠披露所需的系统建设和后续评价也会提高董事会的治理要求。薪酬投票研究则将披露数据转化为可预测的量化投票标准，与股东积极主义季度回顾共同提示：股东权利能否产生约束，仍取决于信息如何进入具体决策程序。

金融监管研究集中于规则边界与救济机制。欧盟可持续金融改革提出简化、协调与可持续目标之间的取舍，缩小适用范围能够减负，却可能改变制度覆盖；联邦委托投票规则调整涉及股东提案进入公司决策程序的条件；市场操纵损害赔偿研究则从私人救济端观察价格形成秩序。三者分别涉及适用范围、参与渠道和违法后的责任配置。

AI相关研究将风险治理与收益分配并置。新加坡金融AI监管强调原则、行业协作、监管科技和执法的组合，企业网络安全文章关注AI放大攻击能力带来的监督压力，数据中心讨论则将视线转向地方社区的谈判条件。由此可见，AI治理既需控制技术及第三方风险，也需明确技术投资的收益如何在企业与受影响群体之间分配。

企业集团研究提示，仅观察单个市场的集中度，可能遗漏相邻市场收购带来的累积力量；纽约联储关于总体通胀与生活成本的讨论，则说明总量指标与个体体验具有不同观察尺度。本期研究因此提示一种方法上的谨慎：评估制度效果时，应结合组织层面、跨市场联系与具体主体处境，避免以单一指标替代完整的机制分析。

#### The Impact of Sustainability Reporting on Directors' ESG Duties: The Disclosure-Duty Feedback Loop
**作者**：Luh Luh Lan
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：Maturation of sustainability reporting has expanded, rather than resolved, the ambiguity surrounding directors’ ESG duties. Reporting standards specify what must be disclosed, but not what directors must do behind the disclosure, while regulatory regimes diverge in scope, materiality and enforcement. The paper explains this paradox through a “disclosure-duty feedback loop”. Because company law regulates process rather than outcomes, mandatory reporting requires boards to establish and oversee systems capable of producing reliable disclosures, drawing them into the governance of the underlying subject matter. Each report then becomes a benchmark against which subsequent board conduct may be assessed, causing the obligation to ratchet upward as disclosure becomes mandatory and assured.

#### Regulating Artificial Intelligence in Finance: Lessons from Singapore
**作者**：Ng Jing Ying, Lin Lin
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：Artificial Intelligence (AI) is rapidly transforming the global financial sector, with widespread adoption across banking, insurance, and other financial services. Singapore exemplifies this trend. Its major financial institutions, including DBS, OCBC, and UOB, have extensively integrated AI and Generative AI (GenAI) to enhance efficiency, productivity, and customer experience. However, the growing use of AI in finance also introduces significant risks related to transparency, data privacy, cybersecurity, fairness, accountability, GenAI hallucinations, and deepfakes. This paper examines Singapore's regulatory approach to governing AI in finance, anchored by the Monetary Authority of Singapore's (MAS) multi-layered strategy that combines principles-based regulation, collaborative industry engagement, supervisory technology (SupTech) adoption, market-enabling measures, and deliberate regulatory gradualism. While Singapore's framework, comprising the FEAT Principles, Project MindForge, and the MAS Guidelines on AI Risk Management, provides a strong foundation, this paper suggests areas for improvement. These include elevating guidelines into enforceable codes, addressing third-party AI risks, leveraging SupTech capabilities, establishing a dedicated AI regulatory sandbox, and ensuring robust enforcement and international cooperation.
**关键词**：AI; Regulation
**主题**：金融监管, 科技、数据与AI
**原文链接**：https://www.ecgi.global/publications/working-papers/regulating-artificial-intelligence-in-finance-lessons-from-singapore

### OBLB（1 篇）

#### The Omnibus I Directive and the EU Sustainable Finance Trilemma
**作者**：Giovanni Strampelli
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：The Omnibus I Directive (Directive (EU) 2026/470 of 24 February 2026) is the first stage of the Commission’s sustainable finance simplification agenda. In aforthcoming article, I argue that it reveals a structural feature of that framework: sustainability, simplification and harmonization cannot be maximized simultaneously, and thus pose a trilemma. Simplification may be achieved by reducing what the rules require of those within scope or the number of undertakings subject to them; harmonization requires a degree of precision that itself raises compliance costs; and sustainability depends on both the content and the breadth of the obligations. The Directive does not resolve that trilemma: simplification and harmonization have in significant part been reconciled by narrowing the class of undertakings subject to the regime rather than by calibrating the obligations imposed. The case for simplification was set out in theLettaandDraghiReports, which assign it different functions: for Letta it is an instrument of harmonization to complete the single market, while Draghi treats it as an autonomous objective and points to the CSRD and the CS3D as paradigmatic instances of excessive burden. The Competitiveness Compass of 29 January 2025 committed the Commission to cut administrative burdens by at least 25% for all undertakings and 35% for SMEs.
**关键词**：
**主题**：金融监管
