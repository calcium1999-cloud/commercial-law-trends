**报告周期**：2026-11-17 — 2026-11-18
**数据来源**：ECGI | Harvard | OBLB | ProMarket | CLS | Yale JREG | Jotwell | BWI | NY Fed | ECB | BoE

## 📊 本周研究趋势概括

本期十一大来源共新增21篇文章，来自ECGI、Harvard、OBLB、ProMarket、CLS和NY Fed。按主分类计，公司治理10篇、金融监管5篇、其他5篇、反垄断1篇；按交叉标签计，公司治理12篇、金融监管7篇。本期没有科技、数据与AI类新增。

公司治理研究共同追问正式治理结构能否约束真实行为。CEO总部迁址论文利用子女异地结婚作为工具变量，发现私人地点偏好可能影响迁址，并伴随负面公告收益、员工流动和内部评价下降；董事会主席研究则强调，董事独立性与绩效之间的相关性可能受内生性、反向因果和社会关系影响。关于机构股东政治活动与联邦采购的研究进一步提示，股东不仅提供资本，也可能传导政治资源；这些均属特定样本和识别设计下的实证结论，不宜直接外推为一般规则。

投票权配置形成另一条主线。股票借贷模型指出，穿透式投票并不必然让结果更贴近基金投资者偏好，因为基金经理仍可通过增加股票出借改变投票权最终归属。与此相邻，规则14a-8的拟议废止引出机构投资者理事会的评论和权力下放论证，多类别表决权结构则体现创始人控制与公众投资者权利之间的制度选择。这里应区分模型推论、拟议规则、评论信和实务指南，不能把规范主张写成已经验证的政策效果。

忠实义务与控制权争议集中体现责任配置问题。特拉华衡平法院在Sarwal案中把向董事会隐瞒重大信息及调查期间销毁公司文件置于忠实义务框架下；两篇Cloudflare评论围绕控制股东通过资本重组维持控制权是否构成自我交易，以及立法改革后法院审查的边界展开争论。前者是具体判决所揭示的责任命题，后两者则是对尚在上诉和制度变动中的法律评价，二者证据层级不同。

金融监管材料呈现从披露到机构监管的多层次变化。SEC关于高管薪酬披露的文件仍是拟议修正；欧盟《可持续金融披露条例》2.0处于三方谈判阶段；OCC实施《GENIUS法案》的框架虽为最终规则，但仍把若干争议问题留待个案处理。纽约联储关于贴现窗口污名的研究关注公开市场如何解读银行借款。反垄断文章则主张强化私人诉讼的社会收益，属于作者基于法律与经验材料提出的政策论证，而非关于所有私人诉讼效果的无条件结论。

其余研究把法律制度放入更广的外部性与分配背景。气候罢工研究发现，靠近抗议地点的公司随后范围一排放下降约12%，但该结果依赖地点比较与局部效应解释；委内瑞拉研究显示制裁期间产量下降同时每桶原油的火炬燃烧增加，提示政策效果可能通过生产结构转化为环境成本。联合国《可转让货物单证公约》试图把可转让单证扩展至多式运输，成效仍取决于批准和商业实践；破产优先顺位研究在既有权利、同类债权人平等与弱势债权人保护之间进行利益衡量；劳动市场研究则检验主观就业预期与实际发生率的偏差。

#### Family First: CEOs' Private Incentives in Corporate Relocations
**作者**：Ran Duchin, Abed El Karim Farroukh, Denis Sosyura
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：Using novel data on executives’ families, we identify CEOs’ private motives in moving corporate headquarters (HQ). CEOs systematically relocate HQs closer to their families over plausible location alternatives. Over 35% of relocations place the new HQ within 50 miles of the CEO’s children and away from production sites and industry clusters. Such relocations entail negative announcement returns, higher employee turnover, and weaker insider reviews. Using an out-of-state marriage of a CEO’s child as an instrument for the CEO’s location preferences, we show that executives’ private incentives affect corporate migration.

#### Stock Lending and Pass-Through Voting
**作者**：Doron Y. Levit, George Nurisso
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：Concerns about conflicts of interest have led to growing calls for asset managers to let fund investors cast votes directly at shareholder meetings. We study this reform, known as pass-through voting, in a model where market participants with heterogeneous preferences lend shares, trade, and vote. We show that it can move voting outcomes further away from investors' preferences, rather than closer, for a reason that has received little attention: the fund's manager retains control over share lending and may lend more once the votes are no longer his to cast. Because the votes attached to lent shares end up in the hands of traders whose preferences differ from those of the fund's investors, pass-through voting can leave these investors worse off, even after accounting for lending revenue. The manager, meanwhile, may embrace the reform to increase lending income. Our analyses inform the debate over mandating pass-through voting, the use of lending fees as a proxy for the value of a vote, and, more broadly, policy at the intersection of shareholder voting and securities lending.
**关键词**：Institutional investors; Markets & liquidity; Voting; Information asymmetry; Stewardship; Shareholder rights
**主题**：公司治理, 金融监管

#### Beyond the Bill of Lading: Can the UN Convention on Negotiable Cargo Documents Transform Global Trade?
**作者**：Shuai Guo
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：The opening for signature of the United Nations Convention on Negotiable Cargo Documents in Accra, Ghana, on 26 October 2026 will mark an important step in the evolution of international trade law. The Convention extends the legal framework for negotiable cargo documents beyond maritime transport, potentially improving access to trade finance and facilitating multimodal commerce. The Convention will enter into force 180 days after the deposit of the tenth instrument of ratification, acceptance, approval or accession. Nevertheless, its success will depend less on the number of signatures than on whether states, courts, banks and carriers can translate its promise into predictable commercial practice. Negotiable maritime bills of lading have long facilitated the sale and financing of goods while they are in transit. Comparable arrangements have been less readily available for goods carried by rail, road or air. This creates a structural disadvantage for inland traders, particularly where goods cross multiple jurisdictions before reaching their destination. The Convention, adopted by the UN General Assembly on 15 December 2025, addresses this problem by establishing a uniform framework for negotiable cargo documents (NCDs) in paper and electronic form, regardless of transport mode. Under Article 7, the holder may exercise the rights represented by the document, while Article 11 governs their transfer.

#### Rethinking Priority Rules in Insolvency
**作者**：Anthony Casey, Aurelio Gurrea-Martínez
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：Insolvency proceedings face two fundamental challenges that, at their core, concern maximizing the value of an insolvent firm and allocating that value among its stakeholders. In our recent article, Rethinking Priority Rules in Insolvency , we focus on the second challenge, examining how jurisdictions structure priority rules and assessing their economic and policy justifications. Priority rules determine who bears losses when companies fail. They also influence decisions made long before insolvency, including investment, risk-taking, financing, and creditor monitoring. Their design therefore has important implications for access to finance, corporate restructuring, and the distribution of losses among stakeholders. Our article argues that two foundational principles of insolvency law—the protection of pre-insolvency entitlements and the equal treatment of similarly situated creditors—should generally be respected. Nevertheless, departures may be justified where they generate economic benefits or protect creditors who cannot adequately protect themselves.
