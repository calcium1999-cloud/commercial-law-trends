# 商业法律研究动向

**报告周期**：2026-10-17 — 2026-10-20
**数据来源**：ECGI | Harvard | OBLB | ProMarket | CLS | Yale JREG | Jotwell | BWI | NY Fed | ECB | BoE

## 📊 本周研究趋势概括

本期十一大来源共新增14篇文章，来自ECGI、Harvard、OBLB、ProMarket与CLS；按主分类计算，公司治理5篇、其他5篇、金融监管3篇、反垄断1篇。Yale、Jotwell、BWI、NY Fed、ECB与BoE在本次增量窗口内无新增。整体研究脉络集中在公司控制权的重新配置、监管边界的数字化扩张，以及价值评估规则的制度基础。

公司治理议题由传统董事会运行延伸至资本结构和退出机制。董事会主席的议程组织、代理投票季中的薪酬表决、日本资本效率改革与零售股东投票机制，讨论的是董事会和股东如何形成有效决策；债权人资本主义与Endeavor估值权争议则表明，当债务融资和现金退出合并改变剩余索取权结构时，公司治理冲突会分别转化为债务合同谈判与公允价值争议。

金融监管与数字基础设施正在形成同一条制度链。DORA把关键ICT服务商纳入欧洲金融监管视野，说明系统重要性已从持牌金融机构外溢到技术依赖；SEC对零售股东投票计划提供“不采取行动”救济，并以临时豁免塑造代币化证券交易场所，又显示监管机关正通过个案性工具试探市场参与和数字交易的新边界。效率与创新收益之外，法律授权、程序正当性和监管套利将成为后续争论焦点。

FTC针对伯莱塔—鲁格少数股权投资中的董事交叉任职执法，将公司治理安排直接转化为竞争法问题。该案提示，少数股权并不当然意味着缺乏竞争影响；董事席位、信息接触和协调渠道可能成为《克莱顿法》第8条审查的独立对象，交易结构设计因而需要同步评估控制权与竞争风险。

跨境破产、许可费率与境外投资安全执法共同凸显了制度中“连接点”和“估值基准”的选择。COMI规则试图以债务人的经济重心协调程序，却可能在价值最大化与分配公平之间制造冲突；许可费率中的循环论证和现金退出合并中的公允价值争议，也都说明既有市场价格并非天然中立。美国财政部OISP首宗处罚则进一步表明，国家安全审查正在把合规判断嵌入资本跨境流动。

两篇芝加哥学派思想史文章为上述制度讨论提供了更深层背景：市场自由并非单一的消极自由概念，反垄断、平等主义、民主参与和商业力量之间的关系始终取决于理论选择。对公司治理和金融监管而言，这意味着所谓效率标准本身也需要说明其分配前提、权力结构与公共责任。

### ECGI（1 篇）

#### The Failure of COMI and the Case for Revisiting the Model Law on Cross-Border Insolvency: Lessons from the Airline Industry
**作者**：Aurelio Gurrea-Martínez, Edward R. Morrison
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：The center of main interests (COMI) is an organizing principle and one of the central pillars of the Model Law on Cross-Border Insolvency enacted by the United Nations Commission on International Trade Law (UNCITRAL) and adopted by more than 60 jurisdictions around the world. By incentivizing the initiation of insolvency proceedings in the debtor's economic "center of gravity" under the threat that proceedings commenced elsewhere may not be recognized, or at least not as foreign main proceedings, COMI seeks to promote predictability and the fair and efficient administration of cross-border insolvencies while limiting forum shopping. Relative to these goals, we show, COMI is a failure. Litigation is common because a firm's COMI is often unclear; forum shopping is rampant, with large corporations viewing COMI as simply a tax that must be paid to local creditors; and the fair and efficient administration of cross-border insolvencies is stymied by COMI, which prevents medium-sized enterprises from selecting the insolvency system that can best maximize creditor recoveries. The trouble with COMI, we argue, is that it confuses value maximization with distributional fairness. Fairness may require honoring the priority rules of the debtor's COMI, but value maximization may require the use of a more efficient insolvency system. We show how to fix this problem.
**关键词**：Bankruptcy; Insolvency; Regulation; Labour; Comparative studies

### OBLB（2 篇）

#### From ‘Too Big to Fail’ Banks to ‘Too Critical to Fail’ Technology Providers?
**作者**：Magda Ositashvili
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：On 18 November 2025, the European Banking Authority, the European Insurance and Occupational Pensions Authority and the European Securities and Markets Authority published the first list of critical information and communication technology third-party providers designated under the Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA). The authorities assessed providers by reference to their systemic importance, their role in supporting critical or important functions and the substitutability of their services. Most immediate commentary has understandably focused on compliance: which providers were designated, what oversight will involve and how financial entities should adapt their governance. Those questions matter. But the designations also raise a more fundamental issue: what happens when financial regulators begin directly overseeing businesses that are not financial institutions? DORA changes the perimeter of financial regulation. For decades, regulators concentrated principally on banks, insurers, investment firms and financial market infrastructures. Under the new framework, European authorities can oversee technology providers whose services underpin the sector. The regulatory spotlight now reaches beyond financial entities to the digital foundations on which they operate.

#### The Law of Debtholder Capitalism
**作者**：Jason Jia-Xi Wu
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：Corporate America is increasingly financed by debt rather than equity. Two market forces drive this shift. On the demand side, private equity has generated an insatiable appetite for debt by acquiring large companies and entire industries through leveraged buyouts. On the supply side, innovations in debt markets—including syndicated lending, private credit, and other forms of nonbank credit—have expanded the debt needed to satisfy that demand. As equity recedes from the center of corporate finance, debt has become a principal instrument through which corporate power is exercised, allocated, and disciplined. Although scholars have long studied how debt influences corporate governance, they have paid less attention to how its changing market structure alters investor conflict. In my article, ‘ The Law of Debtholder Capitalism ’, forthcoming in the Columbia Business Law Review , I argue that this transformation changes the locus, form, and substance of investor conflicts. As companies become heavily debt-financed, disputes that once would have appeared as corporate governance conflicts increasingly appear as disputes over debt contracts. And because leverage amplifies insolvency risk, those disputes often migrate into restructuring negotiations—or, when private ordering fails, into bankruptcy court.
**关键词**：
