# 商业法律研究动向

**报告周期**：2026-11-12 — 2026-11-13
**数据来源**：ECGI | Harvard | OBLB | ProMarket | CLS | Yale JREG | Jotwell | BWI | NY Fed | ECB | BoE

## 📊 本周研究趋势概括

本期十一大来源共新增19篇文章，来自ECGI、Harvard、OBLB、ProMarket、CLS和NY Fed。按主分类计，公司治理9篇、其他5篇、金融监管3篇、反垄断1篇、科技、数据与AI 1篇；计入交叉标签后，公司治理涉及9篇、其他6篇、金融监管3篇、反垄断2篇、科技、数据与AI 1篇。本期共同主线是：治理权、信息和责任如何在投资者、管理者、监管者与组织内部监督者之间重新配置。

股东参与和公司信息制度构成本期最集中的治理议题。穿透式投票能够更细致地表达基金投资者偏好，却也可能把大型机构投资者原本集中的监督力量拆散；特殊股权奖励则要求董事会把例外性激励转化为可向股东说明的理由。SEC拟允许企业逐年选择半年报，以及特拉华州第220条修法后的账簿查阅案件，都表明信息权并非单纯增加或减少披露，而是决定由谁、在何时、以何种程序取得并使用公司信息。

另一些研究把注意力从正式权力转向组织内部更隐蔽的行为约束。总部搬迁可能同时承载企业效率与首席执行官私人生活的考量；企业文化在创立早期形成后，未必会因更换CEO、并购或危机而迅速重置；预算治理研究则指出，让支出者自行证明并评价支出，会产生保护既有额度的结构性激励。三者共同提示，治理评价不能只看董事会是否拥有权力，还要识别人员选择、路径依赖和内部评价机制如何影响权力的实际使用。

公司形式与私募融资材料展示了制度统一和交易定制之间的张力。EU Inc原拟以统一法律形式降低欧洲初创企业跨境经营成本，但谈判中的成员国法回流、共同决策和反洗钱例外可能重新制造碎片化。统一商法理论试图在商业法、金融法、破产法和证券法之间提炼共同问题；延续基金交易中优先股的使用，则说明市场参与者会通过合同安排重新组合流动性、风险和控制。统一规则能否产生实际增量，取决于其是否真正减少接口成本，而不仅是增加新的制度层。

市场监管与风险识别方面，本期材料强调不能把表面信号直接等同于责任结论。知情卖空并非仅集中在家族企业，因此家族控制本身不足以证明更高的信息泄露风险；众筹制度的扩张应以现有投资者保护和市场运行问题得到修正为前提；纽约联储对银行体系脆弱性的年度更新则以四套模型比较不同风险维度。共同的方法论要求是，把相关性、制度标签和真实风险机制分开检验。

技术与公共法材料进一步说明分类和责任边界的重要性。Mixedbread AI案的核心是前高管、董事诉讼中的费用垫付义务，公司名称中的“AI”并不使其成为技术治理案件；英国监管机构关于AI代理的文件才真正涉及自动化行为的竞争法与消费者法责任。哥伦比亚宪法竞争保护案件则把竞争政策与权力分立置于同一层级。无论面对公司名称、技术工具还是宪法原则，分析都应从实际争议和责任机制出发，而不能由标签替代法律判断。

#### Corporate Culture Is Set Early—and It Is Harder to Change Than We Think
**作者**：Kai Li, Yu-Jane Liu, Ruichang Lu, Kaihao Qian, Xiaojun Zhang
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：When companies struggle, boards often reach for a familiar remedy: appoint a new CEO, rewrite the values statement, or reorganize the business. The underlying assumption is that corporate culture can be reset from the top. In our working paper, “Set in Stone: The Persistence and Origins of Corporate Culture,” we suggest a more sobering conclusion: corporate culture is remarkably durable. It is not a reflection of the preferences of the current CEO, nor is it something that disappears after a merger or a crisis. Instead, culture appears to be built into an organization early in its life, and then sustained because the company tends to attract, select, and retain people who fit it. What Do We Mean by Culture? Corporate culture is often discussed in vague terms. Here, it means the shared values and unwritten rules that shape how people behave when contracts, policies, and direct supervision cannot tell them exactly what to do. These values matter because business decisions rarely come with a complete instruction manual. Employees and managers constantly face ambiguous situations: whether to speak up about a problem, how to respond to a customer, or how to balance speed against quality. In those moments, culture can guide decisions when formal rules offer no clear answer.

#### Towards a Unified Theory of Business Law
**作者**：Steven L. Schwarcz, Isabelle Stewart
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：In a new article , we argue that business law’s division into separate disciplines obscures shared problems and generates unnecessary costs. Commercial, financial, bankruptcy, securities, and business-organization law developed largely independently, each responding to particular activities and historical circumstances. Yet these fields repeatedly confront similar difficulties involving information asymmetries, coordination problems, conflicting incentives, and parallel risks. We propose a more integrated legal framework for viewing business law, with the goals of improving efficiency by reducing overlap among the constituent fields, increasing consistency, and more widely disseminating advances across those fields. We mean to merely supplement, not replace, existing regulation, as well as to guide interpretation and encourage useful exchanges across the business-law disciplines. The fragmentation of business law produces overbroad rules, excessive exemptions, and inconsistent treatment. The Investment Company Act’s overbroad definition of securities, for example, inadvertently covers financial assets purchased by securitization vehicles, potentially subjecting those vehicles to excessive regulation even though their use can reduce information asymmetry.

#### Who Evaluates Those Who Spend? Field Evidence on Cross-Functional Budget Governance
**作者**：Thyago Lopes de Lima
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：In most organizations, the people who execute spending are also the people who justify it and assess whether it was well spent. Boards and audit committees review budgets in aggregate, internal audit samples transactions after the fact, and finance teams consolidate figures they did not generate. This is a version of the classic question of who monitors the monitors. What receives far less attention is who independently evaluates spending at the level where spending decisions are actually made. This matters because budget owners have every reason to protect their allocations. A unit that cuts its own costs gains little and risks losing resources in the next cycle. The result is familiar to any CFO: savings programmes that deliver in year one and fade by year three, and budgets that drift upward because no one with a different perspective challenges them. In a recent article in Corporate Ownership & Control , I propose a mechanism designed to close this gap, and in a follow-up field study I examine nine years of evidence from its use in a regulated financial institution.
