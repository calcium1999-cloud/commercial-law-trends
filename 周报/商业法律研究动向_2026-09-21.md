# 商业法律研究动向

**报告周期**：2026-09-18 — 2026-09-21
**数据来源**：ECGI | Harvard | OBLB | ProMarket | CLS | Yale JREG | Jotwell | BWI | NY Fed | ECB | BoE

## 📊 本周研究趋势概括

本期新增5篇文章，来自ECGI、Harvard和OBLB；主分类为公司治理3篇、金融监管1篇、其他1篇。
公司治理研究呈现出鲜明的控制权生命周期视角：风险投资者在IPO后的持续持股与治理安排，说明“上市即退出”的传统叙事需要修正；指定董事责任与CEO非自愿离职研究，则分别从股东提名权和董事会人事权揭示控制权行使的责任边界。
证券诉讼研究聚焦市场欺诈推定在集体诉讼认证阶段的适用。相关论文梳理美国最高法院标准的反复变化，提示程序门槛正在承载过多实体判断，并可能削弱集体救济机制的可预见性。
跨境投资争端研究讨论NAFTA第1134条下临时措施与被诉国家措施之间的界限。First Majestic案表明，仲裁庭能否中止执行，取决于诉请如何界定被争议措施及后续执行行为是否保持法律上的同一性。
总体而言，本期文献共同关注形式权利转化为实际控制的条件：资本退出并不必然终结治理影响，董事席位与管理层更替伴随责任配置，而诉讼和仲裁程序中的争议界定同样会塑造实体保护的范围。

### ECGI（2 篇）

#### Exit After Exit
**作者**：Yifat AranBrian BroughmanElizabeth Pollman
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：Business law and finance scholarship has long treated the initial public offering (IPO) as a clean exit for venture capital. Under the prevailing account, venture capitalists supply capital and governance in the private phase, exit shortly after the IPO, and yield their position to public markets and dispersed shareholders. This assumption underlies doctrinal frameworks and contemporary debates about firms staying private longer and going public with dual-class stock.

#### Supreme Amnesia: The Shifting Standards for Fraud-on-the-Market Class Certification
**作者**：Ann Lipton
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：In a series of cases, beginning with Erica P. John Fund, Inc. v. Halliburton Co., 563 U.S. 804 (2011), and concluding with Goldman Sachs Group, Inc. v. Arkansas Teacher Retirement System, 594 U.S. 113 (2021), the U.S. Supreme Court has offered shifting and conflicting understandings of the fraud-on-the-market presumption and its role in class certification. The confusion has filtered down to the lower courts, where class certification determinations have become wide-ranging inquiries into the merits, untethered from the fundamental question whether class treatment is appropriate. This Essay, written for the ILEP 30 th Anniversary of the PSLRA Symposium, explores how the Supreme Court has created an impossible class certification maze for parties to navigate, and recommends that courts no longer adjudicate fraud-on-the-market at class certification.
**关键词**：Stewardship; Company Law; Regulation; Capital; ESG; Institutional Investors; Technology; Voting; Control; Litigation; Carbon emission; Climate Change
**主题**：金融监管
**原文链接**：https://www.ecgi.global/publications/working-papers/supreme-amnesia-the-shifting-standards-for-fraud-on-the-market-class

**作者**：Brian Massengill, Craig Frame, and Andrew Noreuil, Mayer Brown LLP
**Affiliations**：

This post is based on a Mayer Brown memorandum by Mr. Massengill, Mr. Frame, Mr. Noreuil, and Andrew Stanger, and is part of the Delaware Law Series; links to other posts in the series are available here. Significant stockholders of Delaware corporations may negotiate for the right to designate one or more directors of their choosing to the board. While such a designation right may be extremely valuable, several recent Delaware Chancery Court opinions highlight the risks of personal liability for the designated directors and the stockholders who appoint them. This Legal Update provides guidance on how designated directors and stockholders can navigate these risks, particularly in light of the 2025 amendments to Delaware General Corporation Law (DGCL) §144.

UnderNAFTA Article 1134, a tribunal may grant interim protection but may not order attachment or ‘enjoin the application’ of the measure alleged to constitute the breach. The real problem is one of legal identity: when is enforcement a downstream act that may be restrained, and when is restraining it simply another way of suspending the challenged measure itself?
