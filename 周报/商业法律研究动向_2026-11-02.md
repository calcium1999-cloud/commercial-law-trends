# 商业法律研究动向

**报告周期**：2026-10-31 — 2026-11-02
**数据来源**：ECGI | Harvard | OBLB | ProMarket | CLS | Yale JREG | Jotwell | BWI | NY Fed | ECB | BoE

## 📊 本周研究趋势概括

本期十一大来源共新增10篇文章，来自ECGI、Harvard、OBLB、ProMarket与CLS。按主分类计，公司治理5篇、金融监管4篇、其他1篇。表面议题涉及证券借贷、基金投票、股东提案、IPO执法、预测市场与个性化定价，贯穿其中的共同问题是：信息如何被转化为决策权，程序又如何决定这种权力由谁行使、由谁承担成本。

公司治理研究把投票权从静态权利改写为信息生产机制。证券借贷论文表明，召回股票虽增加可投票股份，却可能减少知情卖空者通过借券需求传递的信息；基金集团研究则检验所谓统一投票是否真的存在。两者共同提示，治理质量不能仅用票数衡量，还要追踪信息从市场进入表决的路径。

积极主义、收购与CEO罢免研究进一步揭示外部约束的双重性。积极投资者既能降低收购中的代理摩擦，也会替代部分纪律性收购；董事交叉任职网络形成的相对绩效基准能够触发CEO罢免，却未必带来更好结果。控制机制是否增加价值，取决于其信息优势、激励结构和因果后果，而非干预本身是否更强。

金融监管文献集中于资本市场的程序性守门权。规则14a-8的改革决定谁能进入公司议程，SEC披露改革决定投资者获得多少可比信息，香港IPO执法则决定发行人和保荐人能否以安排出来的需求通过市场入口。看似技术性的程序规则，实际都在分配发行人、投资者、中介机构与监管者之间的控制权。

预测市场与个性化定价把讨论推进到市场设计的边界。前者要求在信息聚合功能与对灾害、战争等事件的商品化之间划线；后者说明企业利用个体信息定价时，会受到消费者隐瞒偏好和转售套利的反制。法律评价需要同时识别创新带来的信息增益、策略性反应以及被外部化的社会成本。

本期材料在方法上也形成互补：结构模型衡量积极主义与并购的替代关系，代理投票记录检验基金集团内部差异，投资者调查呈现披露需求，执法材料揭示IPO程序风险。对法律研究而言，下一步不只是比较规则文本，而是检验规则是否改变信息流、议程设置、管理层更替和市场准入，并区分形式控制与实际责任。

### ECGI（4 篇）

#### Profiting From Prediction
**作者**：Emilie Aguirre, Gina-Gail Fletcher, Veronica Root Martinez
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：Prediction markets transform uncertain events into profit-making opportunities. By allowing platform users to buy and sell event contracts tied to future outcomes, prediction market platforms convert real-world contingencies into tradable financial products. In the last year, users have traded on uncertainties like elections, corporate events, sports outcomes, weather, public health developments, geopolitical crises, and even war or death. Prediction markets are defended on the basis of providing benefits like information aggregation or better informed risk allocation. In addition to the users attempting to profit from event contracts, prediction market platforms generate revenue by collecting a fee on each transaction. But prediction markets also have potential downsides, including the commodification of events that may involve harm to traders, third parties, and society more broadly. This Article asks when prediction market platforms and their users should be permitted to commodify uncertain, future events and gain financially from users’ predictions. Much of the current debate centers on how to classify prediction markets within existing legal regimes, such as gambling or derivatives law. This Article takes a purposefully broader perspective to develop a more fulsome understanding of the transactions occurring on prediction market platforms. It argues that prediction markets—like many areas of technological and financial innovation—require an assessment of their potential benefits and harms through a lens of bounded commodification. Bounded commodification stands for the principle that there are limits—legal, ethical, and societal—that do and should constrain the private transactions profit-seeking individuals and firms may pursue. Drawing on contract law doctrine and theory, this Article offers a foundational framework for assessing when event contracts should be prohibited, when they should be regulated by state and federal authorities, and when the platforms themselves should be required to adopt ex ante, firm-level internal controls and compliance systems. Prediction markets are here to stay, requiring a principled account of when law should permit or constrain users and platforms to profit from prediction.
**关键词**：Financial regulation; Insider trading; Compliance; Responsible investment; Comparative governance; Banking; Financial crises; Systemic risk; Creditor rights; Enforcement
**主题**：金融监管
**原文链接**：https://www.ecgi.global/publications/working-papers/profiting-from-prediction

#### I Don't Recall: Information and the Governance Tradeoffs in Securities Lending
**作者**：George Nurisso
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：Institutional investors are commonly viewed as facing a tradeoff between revenue and voting power when lending shares. However, lending also generates valuable voting information via the borrowing demand of informed short sellers. I develop a model in which lenders trade off revenue, voting power, and information, and derive novel implications for corporate governance. Contrary to conventional wisdom, expected firm value can suffer when lenders recall shares to cast extra votes, since doing so discourages borrowing ex-ante and thereby reduces the informativeness of those votes; the key governance tradeoff is between a larger number of less informed votes and a smaller number of more informed votes. Thus, share lending and voting are not necessarily substitutes — they can also be complementary.
**关键词**：Voting; Institutional investors; Information asymmetry; Stewardship; Shareholder rights; Board diversity; Climate change
**主题**：公司治理
**原文链接**：https://www.ecgi.global/publications/working-papers/i-dont-recall-information-and-the-governance-tradeoffs-in-securities

#### Shareholder Activism, Takeovers, and Managerial Discipline
**作者**：Francesco Celentano, Oliver Levine
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：We quantitatively assess the role of activism in the market for corporate control by developing and estimating a model featuring both activism and M&A. We find that activism complements M&A, reducing the agency frictions associated with takeovers. However, activism simultaneously crowds out some M&A activity by substituting for disciplinary takeovers. Both the threat of activism and actual activist intervention create shareholder value by improving CEO incentives, while the value from reduced takeover frictions is primarily captured by acquirers. We find that activists have an information advantage, which is critical to overcoming the free rider problem in activist intervention.
**关键词**：Market for control; Activism; Agency theory; Ownership structure; Family firms; Blockholders; Takeovers; Human capital; Mergers; Institutional investors
**主题**：公司治理
**原文链接**：https://www.ecgi.global/publications/working-papers/shareholder-activism-takeovers-and-managerial-discipline

#### How Are CEOs Fired?
**作者**：Jason (Pang-Li) Chen, Ran Duchin, Eliezer Fich
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：CEOs are more likely to be dismissed when their firm is the worst performer within directors’ networks of interlocked boards. The effects persist after including granular fixed effects, controlling for nonlinear, absolute, and industry-relative performance, and in analyses of exogenous director turnovers and falsification tests. Interlock-benchmarked dismissals are followed by performance declines, analyst downgrades, and lower announcement returns, suggesting that these dismissals are not easily explained as value-enhancing responses to poor performance. Our findings highlight a novel channel through which interlocking boards shape governance and shareholder value: implicit relative performance benchmarks.
**关键词**：CEO & succession; Board effectiveness; Family firms; Human capital; Bankruptcy; Responsible investment; Board composition
**主题**：公司治理
**原文链接**：https://www.ecgi.global/publications/working-papers/how-are-ceos-fired

### OBLB（1 篇）

#### Decentralized Voting in Mutual Fund Families
**作者**：Roni Michaely, Matthew Ringgenberg, Silvina Rubio, Irene Yi
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：The academic literature typically assumes that fund families vote in a centralized way, with every fund in the family casting its proxy votes as part of a single, unified bloc. The same assumption underlies recent efforts to decentralize proxy voting at large asset managers. The largest managers have launched ‘voting choice’ programs that shift voting power from fund managers to the funds’ end investors, and several policy proposals would go further by requiring decentralized voting at large fund families. For example, the INDEX Act would require advisers of passive funds holding over 1% of a company’s voting shares to vote as fund investors instruct. In late 2025, the White House reportedly weighed a similar requirement for the largest index-fund managers. But do fund families actually vote as a bloc in practice? Using ISS proxy voting records for US equity mutual funds over the 2006–2023 proxy seasons, we provide the first large-sample examination of voting decentralization within fund families. We document how prevalent decentralized voting is, identify what drives it, and study its implications for corporate governance and for fund clients. Why centralization looks like the norm. At first glance, the data seem to support the conventional view that asset fund families speak with a single voice: funds within the same family disagree in only about 4.2% of institution–proposal observations.

**主题**：公司治理, 金融监管
