## 📊 本周研究趋势概括

本期十一大来源共新增18篇文章，来自ECGI、Harvard、OBLB、ProMarket、CLS和NY Fed。按主分类计，公司治理7篇、其他5篇、金融监管4篇、反垄断2篇；计入交叉标签后，公司治理涉及8篇、金融监管7篇，科技、数据与AI涉及2篇。各文共同追问的是：信息如何转化为治理权、监管约束与制度选择。
治理参与并非越多越好，而取决于信息、流动性和角色成本。大股东进入董事会会获得公司特定信息，却同时承受内幕交易限制；证券出借会减少可投票股份，却可能借由借券需求传递负面信息；非共同基金机构是否投票以及股东参与渠道的分化，则表明正式权利只有在持有人愿意承担信息搜集和介入成本时才会转化为监督。
董事会能力调查与绿色投资研究进一步区分了治理结果和治理质量。董事会可能一面承认专业能力缺口，一面仍优先考虑文化契合；企业在环境政治风险上升时推迟不可逆绿色投资，也未必意味着气候承诺减弱。若薪酬、受托管理或董事评价只观察年度结果，就可能把外部不确定性误判为内部治理失败。
金融监管文章集中检验披露和豁免如何改变信息生产。Rule 14Ad-1使机构管理人薪酬投票可被观察，Rule 14a-8之争触及股东提案的程序价值，审计豁免则可能在降低合规成本的同时削弱融资受限企业的可信度。SEC年度执法数据提供的是执行强度的结果指标，但不能替代对具体信息机制和企业行为反应的分析。
公司法与跨境制度设计展示了形式统一和实际约束之间的落差。EU Inc若重新引入成员国公司法、反洗钱和共同决策规则，统一公司形式的可扩展性就会被削弱；COMI既可能约束择地破产，也可能只给大型企业增加一项可支付的“COMI税”；美国对外投资规则则说明资本配置还会受到国家安全边界的重塑。
数字市场和宏观金融研究共同强调规则的适应性。Meta“同意或付费”决定若只关注个人同意，可能忽略《数字市场法》的竞争目标；附条件罚款试图解决行为性救济随时间失效的问题。信用卡逾期的收入差距和150年货币政策比较则提醒，监管成效必须相对于借款人结构、经济冲击和当时可行的政策选择来评价，而不能只比较表面结果。

#### Greening on Hold: Environmental Political Risk and the Reallocation of Corporate Investment
**作者**：Matthew Gustafson, Ugur Lel, Zhongling (Danny) Qin
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：Boards and institutional investors increasingly monitor firms’ climate commitments through engagement, stewardship, and executive pay tied to environmental targets. We study how environmental political risk (E-PRisk) shapes the green investment decisions that these monitors evaluate. Combining conference-call-based firm-level E-PRisk with global green and non-green investment data, we find that higher E-PRisk predicts subsequently lower green and higher non-green investment. Both reverse as E-PRisk resolves. When close elections occur in the intervening year, E-PRisk persists longer and green reversals weaken. The effects concentrate in capital expenditures, and among financially constrained firms or those with less redeployable assets. Together, the findings are consistent with a real option mechanism in which firms postpone irreversible green projects and redirect capacity toward other projects, making investment composition a key adjustment margin. For corporate governance, the results suggest that a temporary drop in green investment under policy uncertainty need not signal weak commitment, which matters for how boards and investors judge climate commitments. Oversight, stewardship, and climate-linked pay that evaluate green investment year by year may therefore penalize delays that reflect policy risk rather than weak commitment. More broadly, the results show how the political environment shapes the capital-allocation decisions that corporate governance is meant to monitor.

#### The Failure of COMI and the Case for Revisiting the UNCITRAL Model Law on Cross-Border Insolvency
**作者**：Aurelio Gurrea Martínez, Edward Morrison
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：The debtor’s centre of main interests (COMI) is one of the central pillars of modern cross-border insolvency frameworks and a key concept of the Model Law on Cross-Border Insolvency adopted by the United Nations Commission on International Trade Law (UNCITRAL) and implemented in more than 60 jurisdictions worldwide. By incentivizing the initiation of insolvency proceedings in the debtor’s economic 'center of gravity' under the threat that proceedings commenced elsewhere may not be recognized, or at least not as foreign main proceedings, COMI is thought to promote predictability, limit forum shopping, and facilitate the fair and efficient administration of cross-border insolvencies. COMI is a failure. In a recent article , we show that COMI a costly and inefficient concept that should be abolished or, at the very least, fundamentally revisited. Our analysis draws on practitioner interviews, judicial decisions, and a dataset of financially distressed airlines. Our findings suggest that the costs and effects of COMI vary considerably depending on a company’s size. For large multinational companies, COMI is costly but largely irrelevant. These firms can often choose where to initiate insolvency proceedings regardless of their COMI. However, doing so may require paying what we call a 'COMI tax'. This may involve paying local creditors in full, conducting parallel proceedings, or avoiding recognition of the foreign proceedings in the company’s home jurisdiction.

#### Conceptual Sandboxes: Testing Legal Concepts Before It’s Too Late
**作者**：Claudio Novelli, Luciano Floridi, Sofia Ranchordás, Antonino Rotolo, Leendert Van der Torre, Liuwen Yu
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：When many hands write a law, something odd can happen to its vocabulary. Civil-society groups, industry lobbyists, technical experts, and legislators share a drafting table for years and eventually sign off on a text they all believe they understand. Yet the agreement often rests on the quiet trick that parties have converged on a word without converging on its meaning. The hard conversation about what ‘deployer’ or ‘genetic information’ presupposes, and what using them legally entails, has been postponed. We argue that it is an overlooked pathology of multi-stakeholder lawmaking. Carl Schmitt saw a constitutional version in 1928; Weimar compromises that satisfied opposing camps by leaving the real decision open. As many years of literature on this aspect show, vagueness can be a feature of law, and legislators routinely hand specification to courts and agencies on purpose. The trouble starts when nobody notices that specification has been deferred. Parties walk away believing they share a common meaning, and reconciling their divergent assumptions falls to downstream actors, eg, implementing agencies, litigants, and regulated firms, who were never part of the inclusive process and lack its breadth of knowledge. Multi-stakeholder drafting, whose entire justification is the integration of dispersed expertise, defeats itself the moment it seems to succeed.
