### OBLB（1 篇）

#### How Public Capital Shapes Venture Decisions in China
**作者**：Jun Yan
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：China’s rules for government investment funds ask public capital to serve national strategies and designated industries while telling government agencies not to interfere through administrative means in individual investment decisions. The balance became more explicit in January 2026, when the National Development and Reform Commission introduced a formal system for evaluating the investment direction of government funds . Policy alignment accounts for 60 per cent of the score, production-layout objectives for 30 per cent and execution capacity for 10 per cent. Stronger results can bring project referrals, financing support and cooperation with other public funds; evaluation can also affect fund contributions, management fees and profit distribution. That creates a feedback loop. Policy objectives matter not only when a fund is established. The portfolio a manager builds can affect the resources and economics available to the fund afterwards.

#### Anti-Monopoly as Industrial Policy: The Chicago School Versus Adam Smith
**作者**：Richard R. John
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：Jolted by the Great Depression of the 1930s, Thurman Arnold devised an anti-monopoly industrial policy for the United States Justice Department that breathed fresh life into a then-moribund Sherman Anti-Trust Act. Arnold’s jurisprudence built on Adam Smith’s discovery that market-making could promote national security and forestall civil unrest. Smith’s insights had informed the industrial policy of Alexander Hamilton, who laid the groundwork in the 1790s for the country’s economic development. Edward H. Levi brought Arnold’s pragmatic anti-monopolism to the University of Chicago. Here, ironically, Arnoldian jurisprudence would be transmogrified into the doctrinaire company-over-country market fundamentalism of the Chicago School of Economics. Today, writes Richard R. John, as we envision for the 21st century a new pro-market, pro-freedom, pro-national security industrial policy, it is worth recalling how U. S. economic development took the form that it did, why the Chicago School repudiated the enduring wisdom of Adam Smith, and what this portends for the world of today.
**关键词**：
**主题**：反垄断
**原文链接**：https://www.promarket.org/2026/10/20/anti-monopoly-as-industrial-policy-the-chicago-school-versus-adam-smith/
