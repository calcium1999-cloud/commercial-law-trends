# 商业法律研究动向

**报告周期**：2026-10-15 — 2026-10-16
**数据来源**：ECGI | Harvard | OBLB | ProMarket | CLS | Yale JREG | Jotwell | BWI | NY Fed | ECB | BoE

## 📊 本周研究趋势概括

本期十一大来源共新增20篇文章，来自ECGI、Harvard、OBLB、ProMarket、CLS、BWI、NY Fed与BoE。按主分类计算，金融监管7篇、其他5篇、公司治理4篇、科技、数据与AI 4篇；Yale、Jotwell与ECB在本次增量窗口内无新增。整体脉络由公司内部问责、资本市场准入与执法、系统性风险以及AI驱动的决策与测量四条线索交织而成。

公司治理研究集中于公司自治边界与问责机制的重新配置。公司章程中的强制仲裁把股东救济渠道纳入注册地竞争；SEC拟废除Rule 14a-8、Glass Lewis拟重构代理投票准则，则分别从公法规则和市场中介两个方向改变股东表达与薪酬监督。与此同时，长期限制性股票单位和公司法上的恶意理论提醒我们，放宽程序约束并不会消除董事、高管及控制结构的责任问题，反而要求更清楚地区分激励设计、监督失灵与明知违法。

金融监管材料呈现出资本形成与市场约束并行推进的张力。欧盟证券监管的制度基础、美国IPO与后续发行数据、零售基金准入改革都指向扩大融资和投资渠道；证券执法效果与Rule 14a-8改革则强调信息质量、反欺诈与投资者参与仍是市场可信度的前提。银行监管还受到选举与机构人事变化影响，说明监管连续性不仅取决于规则文本，也取决于政治控制权和行政执行能力。

养老金受托人的系统性风险问题把微观信义义务与宏观审慎监管直接连接起来。单个受托人采用负债驱动投资策略可能完全可辩护，但大量机构同时采取相似策略会放大抵押品压力和资产抛售。由此产生的核心问题不是受托人能否考虑气候、流动性等系统风险，而是当集体行为本身制造风险时，法律是否应从“允许考虑”转向“要求管理”。

AI相关研究显示，人工智能既是治理对象，也是监管与宏观分析工具。董事会使用自主智能体AI时，合理依赖专业知识不能退化为转移判断责任；英格兰银行则利用大型语言模型从企业走访记录和货币政策委员会逐字稿中提取定价与政策叙事。结合AI投资对美国经常账户的研究，本期材料共同提示：评估AI的法律经济影响，需要同时考察责任归属、测量方法和宏观传导机制。

其余研究补充了制度运行的经验与思想维度。圣保罗法院数据检验了巴西仲裁司法审查是否趋严，私募基金管理人的信息沟通研究揭示策略性披露问题，纽约联储的人均营收分析区分生产率增长与企业份额变化；围绕亚当·斯密、芝加哥学派与民主的讨论，则提醒市场竞争概念不能脱离道德约束和政治制度而被简单理解。

### OBLB（2 篇）

#### Permitted or Required? Pension Trustees and the System-Level Risk They Create
**作者**：Lincoln Booth
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：Twice in two years the UK Government has described the fiduciary duty of pension trustees as permissive. In April 2025 the Department for Work and Pensions said that the findings of the Law Commission review of investment intermediaries ‘reflect the permissive nature of trustee fiduciary duty’, and the Government was ‘not currently considering any change to the law’. In January 2026 the Department committed to guidance giving trustees ‘clarification and practical support on their ability to take account of system level risks, such as climate related risks’. Both statements answer a real question. Neither answers the harder one, which arises when the system-level risk is produced by the investment strategies of trustees themselves. The 2022 gilt market episode is the clearest recent illustration. Defined benefit schemes used liability-driven investment strategies to hedge long-term interest rate and inflation exposure. Sharp increases in gilt yields triggered collateral and margin calls, and schemes sold assets to meet them. The Bank of England later found that many liability-driven investment funds were not sufficiently prepared for movements of that size, and that stress testing had not adequately considered what would happen if many market participants reacted at the same time. The strategy adopted by any single scheme was defensible. The damage came from the aggregate. What followed is equally instructive for lawyers.

#### Is Brazil Getting Tougher on Arbitral Awards? Evidence from the Court of Justice of São Paulo
**作者**：Camilo Zufelato, Victor Dantas de Maio Martinez, Fernando Barroso
**Affiliations**：
**摘要**：Arbitration is the resolution mechanism of choice for high-value commercial disputes in Brazil, and São Paulo state is its centre. Its court, the Court of Justice of São Paulo (TJSP), is the largest in Brazil and hears more arbitration-related cases than any other in the country. It is also highly specialised, with first-instance courts dedicated to business and arbitration disputes and two chambers reserved for business law-related appeals . A few years ago, the Brazilian legal press suggested that the relationship between the TJSP and arbitration was souring. Losing parties appeared to be challenging arbitral awards more frequently, and the courts appeared increasingly willing to set them aside. Nonetheless, relevant voices in Brazilian arbitration described the debate as ‘ a lot of smoke and little fire ’. Our empirical research set out to test what the TJSP’s decisions actually show, first for 2011–2022 and then, in an update, up to August 2025. As in many other jurisdictions, judicial review is not an appeal on the merits. Brazilian law limits judicial review of arbitral awards, protecting the finality bargained for by commercial parties. An award may only be annulled on the grounds set out in Article 32 of the Brazilian Arbitration Act (Lei nº 9.307/1996), either through an independent set-aside action brought within 90 days of notification of the award or as a defence in enforcement proceedings.
